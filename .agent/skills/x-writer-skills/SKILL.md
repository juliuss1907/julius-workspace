## CT Voice Guidelines

### Core Principles

Crypto Twitter (CT) voice is:
1. **Chill but informed** — Casual language, serious insights
2. **Self-aware** — Acknowledge mistakes, don't pretend to know everything
3. **Community-oriented** — "We" over "I", fren over expert
4. **Sarcastic when appropriate** — Mock hype, celebrate real building
5. **Crypto-native** — Always tie back to crypto, even tangential topics

### Tone Spectrum

CT content sits on 3 axes:

**Axis 1: Formality**

Casual ←→ Professional"ser this is based" ←→ "This represents a significant development"

**Default:** Casual (70% of content)  
**Use professional:** Regulatory topics, serious security issues

**Axis 2: Emotion**

Chill ←→ Hype"kinda interesting ngl" ←→ "THIS CHANGES EVERYTHING"

**Default:** Chill (80% of content)  
**Use hype:** Major launches, genuine breakthroughs (rare)

**Axis 3: Stance**

Skeptical ←→ Optimistic"another rug incoming" ←→ "wagmi frens"

**Default:** Skeptical-neutral (60% of content)  
**Use optimistic:** Real progress, community wins

## CT Tie-In Rule

**Rule:** Every piece of CT content must connect to crypto in at least one way.

### Connection Types

**Direct:** Topic is crypto-native
- "How to use Uniswap V4"
- "Solana vs Ethereum fee comparison"
- "Why most airdrops fail"

**Indirect:** Topic is general, but framed through crypto lens
- ❌ "How to use Obsidian for productivity"
- ✅ "How to use Obsidian to track alpha and research plays"

**Metaphorical:** Use crypto concepts to explain non-crypto topics
- "Building a personal brand is like liquidity mining — you provide value, earn attention"
- "Learning AI is like farming airdrops — early movers get disproportionate rewards"

### When Tie-In Is Hard

If topic genuinely has no crypto angle:
1. Ask: "Why does CT care about this?"
2. If no good answer → wrong audience, don't force it
3. If there's a reason → make that reason explicit in opening

**Example:**

Topic: "How to learn AI in 30 days"

❌ Bad: Just write generic AI guide✅ Good: "AI agents are eating crypto. Here's how to build one in 30 days."

## Slang Usage

See [slang.md](slang.md) for full CT lexicon.

### Usage Rules

**Frequency:**
- **Posts:** 1-2 slang terms per tweet (max)
- **Threads:** 2-3 slang terms per thread (spread across tweets)
- **Articles:** 5-10 slang terms per article (natural distribution)

**Placement:**
- **Opening:** Establish CT voice early ("gm frens", "ser")
- **Mid-content:** Emphasize points ("this is actually based")
- **Closing:** Community vibe ("wagmi", "LFG")

**Avoid:**
- Forcing slang where it doesn't fit
- Using slang reader might not know without context
- Mixing incompatible slang (formal + degen in same sentence)

### Slang Tiers

**Tier 1: Universal CT** (everyone knows)
- gm, ser, fren, anon, wagmi, ngmi, degen, based

**Tier 2: Common CT** (most know)
- aped in, rugged, jeet, cope, pilled, send it, nuke

**Tier 3: Niche CT** (context-dependent)
- midcurve, gigabrain, skill issue, mog, grok

**Default:** Use Tier 1 freely, Tier 2 with context, Tier 3 sparingly.

## Emoji Usage

See [emoji.md](emoji.md) for full CT emoji guide.

### Usage Rules

**Frequency:**
- **Posts:** 2-5 emojis per tweet
- **Threads:** 3-6 emojis per thread (not every tweet)
- **Articles:** Minimal (headers or emphasis only)

**Placement:**
- **Hook:** Grab attention (🚨, 🧵, 👀)
- **Emphasis:** Key points (🔥, ⚡, 💎)
- **CTA:** Engagement (🔄, 🔖, 👇)

**CT-Specific Emojis:**
- 🧠 (alpha/research)
- 🫡 (salute/respect)
- 🤝 (collab/partnership)
- 💀 (ngmi/rekt)
- 🧵 (thread indicator)

**Avoid:**
- Generic corporate emojis (🎉, 🌟, ✨) unless ironic
- Emoji spam (5+ in one line)
- Emoji as word replacement in serious content

## Universal Rules (All X Content)

### Character Limits
- **Post:** 280 characters max
- **Thread tweet:** 280 characters max per tweet
- **Article:** No hard limit, but 800-2500 words optimal

### Formatting
- **Line breaks:** Use for readability (max 3-4 per tweet)
- **Bold/Italic:** Not supported on X, use CAPS sparingly for emphasis
- **Links:** Shorten when possible, place at end

### Engagement Optimization
- **Hook first 10 words:** Grab attention immediately
- **CTA required:** Every piece needs call-to-action
- **Question or cliffhanger:** Keep reader engaged

### Hashtags
- **Posts:** 0-2 hashtags (end of tweet)
- **Threads:** 0-1 hashtag (final tweet only)
- **Articles:** 2-4 hashtags (end of article)

**CT hashtag strategy:**
- Use established tags (#DeFi, #Solana, #Bitcoin)
- Avoid generic tags (#crypto, #blockchain)
- Project-specific tags when relevant ($TOKEN, #ProjectName)
