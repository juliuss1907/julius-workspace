## Usage Guidelines

### Frequency Rules

**Posts (single tweets):**
- 1-2 slang terms max
- Prefer Tier 1
- Use Tier 2 only if audience clearly knows

**Threads:**
- 2-3 slang terms per thread (not per tweet)
- Spread across tweets (not clustered)
- Mix Tier 1 + Tier 2

**Articles:**
- 5-10 slang terms per article
- Natural distribution (not forced)
- Can use Tier 3 if explained

### Placement Strategy

**Opening (establish CT voice):**

✅ "gm frens, let's talk about X"✅ "ser, this is important"❌ "Good morning everyone, today we'll discuss..."


**Mid-content (emphasis):**

✅ "this is actually based"✅ "if you don't understand this, ngmi"❌ "this is good" (too bland for CT)


**Closing (community vibe):**

✅ "wagmi frens 🫡"✅ "LFG, let's build"❌ "Thank you for reading" (too formal)

## Context-Dependent Usage

### Serious Topics (Use Less Slang)

When discussing:
- Security vulnerabilities
- Regulatory issues
- Major hacks or losses

**Approach:**
- Reduce slang to 0-1 per piece
- Keep Tier 1 only (gm, ser, fren)
- Maintain CT voice through tone, not slang

**Example:**

❌ "ser, this exploit is ngmi, full degen mode"✅ "This exploit is serious. Here's what happened:"


### Hype Topics (Use More Slang)

When discussing:
- Major launches
- Community wins
- Memes and culture

**Approach:**
- Increase slang to 3-5 per piece
- Mix Tier 1 + Tier 2
- Match community energy

**Example:**

✅ "gm frens, we're so back. LFG! 🚀"✅ "aped in at $0.10, now $10. wagmi ser"

## Slang Combinations

### Natural Combos (Use Together)

These pairs/groups work well together:

- **gm + frens** — "gm frens 🫡"
- **ser + based** — "ser, this is based"
- **wagmi + LFG** — "wagmi frens, LFG!"
- **degen + aped in** — "full degen, aped in"
- **ngmi + cope** — "pure cope, ngmi"

### Awkward Combos (Avoid)

These don't mix well:

- ❌ **ser + fuck** — Tone clash (respectful + aggressive)
- ❌ **wagmi + ngmi** — Contradictory
- ❌ **gm + rekt** — Mood clash (positive + negative)
- ❌ **based + cope** — Contradictory (authentic + denial)

## Slang Anti-Patterns

### ❌ Forced Slang

"gm ser frens, this based alpha is wagmi ngmi degen mode"

**Why bad:** Slang spam, unreadable.

### ❌ Explaining Slang Mid-Content

"This is based (which means authentic and correct)"

**Why bad:** Breaks flow. If reader doesn't know, wrong audience.

### ❌ Mixing Formal + Slang Awkwardly

"In accordance with regulatory frameworks, ser, this is ngmi"

**Why bad:** Tone clash.

### ✅ Natural Slang

"gm frens. Been researching X for weeks. Here's what I found:"

**Why good:** Natural, establishes CT voice, doesn't overdo it.

## Regional Variations

CT is global, but some slang has regional flavor:

**US/Western CT:**
- More "LFG", "send it", "based"
- Less formal overall

**Asian CT:**
- More "ser", "fren", "gm"
- Slightly more respectful tone

**Default:** Use universal Tier 1 slang. Avoid region-specific unless targeting specific audience.
