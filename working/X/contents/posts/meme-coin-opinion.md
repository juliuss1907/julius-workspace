## Raw thought process

Meme coins strip away the pretense. Every cycle, people mock meme coin traders while holding bags of "AI infrastructure tokens" that will never ship a product. At least the meme coin buyer knows the game.

The real critique of meme coins — rugs, insider dumps, extraction — is valid. But that's a critique of *bad actors exploiting low-barrier markets*, not of meme coins inherently. The same extraction happens in "legitimate" token launches, just with better storytelling.

If you're going to gamble, respect the people who do it honestly.
